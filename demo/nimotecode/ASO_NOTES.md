# App Store discovery and conversion notes

The screenshot sequence is optimized for recognition in the first three slots: mobile IDE, SSH, AI coding. Apple says one to three portrait screenshots may appear in search results when no preview video is available, so these three carry the core story. Screenshots can improve product understanding; they do not guarantee rankings or downloads.

For search discoverability, review the actual App Store Connect name, subtitle, and keyword field alongside the screenshots. Candidate terms supported by the visible screens include mobile IDE, code editor, SSH, terminal, Git, AI coding, and remote development. Choose terms that accurately match the shipping product and fit Apple's current field limits.

For conversion, use Apple's product page optimization to compare this sequence with alternate first-three copy or artwork. Observe conversion in App Analytics before choosing a winner.

Sources: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/ and https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/
